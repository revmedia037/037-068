Adorable Puppy Care

Raising a puppy requires patience, consistency, and lots of love. Start with basic obedience training to establish good behavior early. Socialize your puppy with other dogs and people to prevent fearfulness. Feed high-quality puppy food to support growth and development. Schedule regular vet checkups for vaccinations and health monitoring. Provide safe chew toys to ease teething discomfort. Enjoy the playful moments—they grow up fast!
